## Research caveats — read before quoting these numbers

1. **Vendor-published comparisons are marketing.** Most "best email verifier 2026" articles are written by competitors or affiliates. The rankings are unreliable; the *pricing data* is usually accurate but goes stale fast.
2. **Hunter's benchmark is the best public data available, but Hunter ran it** and acknowledged its own ground-truth labels may have favoured it. Trust the overall accuracy *level* (~60–70%), be sceptical of the *ranking*.
3. **Market size estimates vary 2×** between analyst firms because they define the category differently. Use the range, never a single figure.
4. **Reddit findings here are second-hand**, drawn from articles summarising community sentiment rather than direct thread reads. **Before you build, go read r/coldemail and r/emailmarketing directly for a week.** That's primary research and it's free.
5. **All prices are July 2026 snapshots.** Hetzner alone raised cloud prices 30–37% in April 2026. Re-verify before committing.
6. **The one number you should generate yourself** is your own accuracy benchmark on your own ground-truth list. Every other accuracy figure in this pack is somebody else's marketing.
