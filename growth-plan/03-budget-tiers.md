# 03 — Budget Tiers: $0 to $2,000/month

You asked for every possibility from lowest budget upward. Here they are, with real 2026 prices.

**Rule before you spend anything: do not move up a tier until the tier below it has produced a paying client.** Every tier here is self-funding — you climb it with revenue, not savings.

---

## TIER 0 — $0/month

**This tier alone can realistically produce your first $2,000–4,000.** Do not skip it because it's unglamorous. It is the highest ROI section of this entire document.

| Action | Why it's free | Expected yield |
|---|---|---|
| **Win-back campaign** to every past client (24 months) | You have their emails already | 1–3 clients. Highest close rate of any channel |
| **Dead-lead resurrection** — everyone who ever replied, quoted, or ghosted | Same | 1–2 clients |
| **Raise your prices** | It's a decision, not a purchase | +30–100% per client |
| **Pick a niche** | Same | 2–3x reply rates downstream |
| **Write + rehearse the sales script** | [`05-sales-playbook.md`](05-sales-playbook.md) | Doubles close rate |
| **Ask every current + past client for 1 referral** | Direct ask, by phone | ~20% of asks produce an intro |
| **Partner outreach** — web designers, freelance devs, PR/branding agencies, copywriters who *don't* do SEO | They have clients asking for SEO and no way to serve them | 1 partner = recurring deal flow |
| **White-label pitches to other agencies** — 20 manual emails | Manual = free | 1 partner = $2,000–5,000/mo |
| **Free Gmail/Outlook manual outreach** — 20/day from your real inbox | No infra cost, best deliverability you'll ever have | 400/mo at high placement |
| **Fix your LinkedIn sequence** (no-pitch connect → post-accept DM) | Re-sequencing your existing tool | 10–11% reply vs your current ~0% |
| **Reply to every inbound by hand within 5 minutes** | Free | Speed-to-lead is the single biggest close-rate variable in the industry |

**Time cost:** ~3 hours/day for 2 weeks.
**Realistic outcome:** $2,000–4,000/mo added.

> If you do nothing else in this document, do Tier 0. It requires zero money and it addresses the two most broken things (price, sales process) plus the cheapest revenue that exists (people who already paid you).

## TIER 1 — ~$120/month (LOWEST PAID TIER)

Turns on real cold email volume. **This is the tier I'd actually start at.**

| Item | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Sending domains × 4 (variants of your brand, **never your main domain**) | Cloudflare / Namecheap | ~$4/mo ($48/yr) |
| Mailboxes × 12 (3 per domain) | Maildoso-style shared infra @ $2–4/inbox | $24–48/mo |
| Sequencer + warmup | Instantly Growth (annual) — unlimited email accounts + unlimited warmup | ~$38/mo |
| Lead data | Instantly Lead Finder (1,000 leads/mo) | ~$38/mo |
| Email verification | Bounce-check before every send (non-negotiable — bounces must stay under 2%) | ~$10/mo |
| **Total** | | **~$115–135/mo** |

**Capacity:** 12 mailboxes × 25/day = **300 emails/day = ~6,600/month.**
**Expected at benchmark:** 6,600 sends → ~230 replies at 3.5% → ~50 positive → **15–25 meetings/mo** → **3–5 clients/mo** at a 20% close.

**Notes:**
- You already have 15 mailboxes. **Audit those first** — if they're on decent infra with correct DNS, your only new cost here is the sequencer and lead data (~$76/mo). Don't buy what you have.
- Free alternative for lead data: Apollo's free plan is **1,200 credits per year**, which is ~100 leads/month. Useful for testing your ICP, useless for volume. Don't build on it.
- Unlimited-mailbox pricing (Instantly Growth) is why it beats per-seat tools at your stage.

## TIER 3 — ~$800/month

Adds dedicated infrastructure and paid inbound.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Everything in Tier 2 | ~$370 |
| **Dedicated cold email infrastructure** — flat-rate unlimited inboxes on dedicated US IPs with automated SPF/DKIM/DMARC (Inframail-class, ~$129/mo) or a managed dedicated server (Mailreef-class, from ~$240/mo for 150+ mailboxes) | $129–240 |
| Google Ads — bottom-funnel only. Bid on *"[niche] seo agency"*, *"[niche] marketing agency near me"*, competitor names | $200–300 |
| Apollo Basic (better firmographic filtering + intent) | $49 |
| Better contractor / editor for delivery quality | reinvest |
| **Total** | **~$750–950/mo** |

**Why dedicated IPs matter here:** shared IP pools mean your sender reputation is affected by strangers' behaviour. At Tier 1–2 volumes that's an acceptable risk. Above ~500 emails/day it isn't — one bad actor on your pool can kill your placement overnight.

**On Google Ads:** with a small budget, only bid on high-intent bottom-funnel terms. Someone searching "roofing seo agency" is in-market today. Never run broad awareness campaigns at this budget — you'll spend $300 and learn nothing.

## TIER 4 — ~$2,000/month

Only enter this once you're consistently above $8,000/mo. This tier buys **time**, not leads.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Everything in Tier 3 | ~$850 |
| **Part-time appointment setter / VA** — handles reply triage, follow-ups, booking. This is the single best purchase at this level | $400–800 |
| Paid ads scaled | $500 |
| Content/authority — 4 niche articles/mo for inbound + sales collateral | $200 |
| Premium data (verified mobiles for cold calling) | $150 |
| **Total** | **~$2,100–2,500/mo** |

**Why the setter is the key hire:** at $10k/mo your bottleneck stops being leads and becomes *your hours*. A setter handling reply triage and booking gives you back 15–20 hrs/week to do the two things only you can do: run sales calls and keep clients happy. That's the constraint you identified yourself.

## Where NOT to spend money (common ways agency owners burn cash at your stage)

| Don't buy | Why |
|---|---|
| A bought Upwork/Fiverr account | Permanent ban + escrow loss + 24-month non-circumvention. Bad trade, not just a rule violation |
| A "done-for-you lead gen" agency | $1,500–3,000/mo, and they'll run the exact playbook in this document. You cannot outsource sales at $1k MRR |
| Expensive all-in-one CRM (HubSpot etc.) | You have 2 clients. A spreadsheet is genuinely better until ~20 |
| More paid tools before Tier 0 is done | You have a process problem, not a tooling problem |
| Broad-match Google Ads / "brand awareness" | You cannot buy awareness on $300 |
| Instagram lead-gen API credits | Lowest-intent B2B channel on your list. Costs real money, converts worst — see [`04-channels.md`](04-channels.md#7-instagram--lowest-priority) |
| A new website for yourself | Yours is done. It is not why you aren't closing |
