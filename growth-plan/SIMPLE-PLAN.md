## Mistake 5 — Your volume is too small AND your email settings are probably broken

### The volume problem

| Step | Rate | Result from 600 |
|---|---|---|
| Reached inbox | 80% | 480 |

**You got exactly what the math said you would get.** Cold email is not broken. Your test was too small to show anything.

To get 10 meetings per month you need **3,000 to 6,000 emails per month**. That is 100 to 200 per day.

You have 15 email accounts. 15 × 25 per day = 375 per day. **You already have enough. You are just not sending.**

### The settings problem — CHECK THIS FIRST

In 2026 Google, Yahoo and Microsoft changed the rules. Before, bad emails went to spam. **Now they are deleted completely. They never arrive anywhere.**

| Rule | What you must have |
|---|---|
| SPF, DKIM, DMARC | All must pass and match |
| DMARC setting | Must be `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`. **`p=none` does not work anymore** |
| One-click unsubscribe | Must have it. Microsoft treats missing = spam |
| Spam complaints | Under 0.3% |
| Bounces | Under 2% |
| Inbox placement | 80% or higher |

Most people leave DMARC at `p=none` because that is the default. If yours is `p=none` and you have no unsubscribe button, **many of your 600 emails were deleted before anyone saw them.**

So you don't even know your real reply rate. Your test was not valid.

**Check this today. Before you send one more email.**

# PART 6 — THE SALES SCRIPT

> *"the meeting part is difficult. I never prepare. What should I speak?"*

This part answers that. Print it. Keep it open on a second screen during calls. **Nobody can tell.**

## The mindset that fixes everything

**A sales call is not a performance. You are not there to impress them. You are there to find their problem.**

A doctor does not walk in and sell surgery. He asks questions until the problem is clear. Then the treatment sells itself.

**If you are talking more than 30% of the call, you are doing it wrong.**

This one idea removes most of your fear. You do not need to know what to **say**. You only need to know what to **ask**. All the questions are written below.

---

## Before the call — 10 minutes. Never skip.

Never take a call with no preparation. 10 minutes is the difference between closing and "let me think about it."

**Find these 5 things and write them on one page:**

1. Their Google position for their main keyword — and who is above them
2. Their Google Business Profile: rating, review count, last post, photos
3. Their website: is it fast? Mobile friendly? Does it have a clear button to call?
4. Are they running Google Ads right now?
5. **Their top competitor's numbers** — reviews, ranking, what they do differently

**Then write 3 sentences. Just 3. That is your whole script:**

> 1. "You are position 8 for '[keyword]'. [Competitor] is position 1."
> 2. "They have 87 reviews. You have 11."
> 3. "About 480 people search that every month in [City]."

That is it. Those 3 facts will sell better than anything you could say about yourself.

---

## The call — 30 minutes

| Part | Time | Who talks |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Set the agenda | 2 min | You |
| 2. Questions | 12 min | **Them (90%)** |
| 3. Show the gap | 4 min | Both |
| 4. What you would do | 5 min | You |
| 5. Price | 2 min | You, then **silence** |
| 6. Objections | 3 min | Both |
| 7. Close | 2 min | You |

---

### Part 1 — Set the agenda (2 min)

Say this almost exactly:

> "Thanks for making time, [Name]. Here is how I would like to use the next 30 minutes.
>
> First 10 to 15 minutes I will just ask you questions about the business — where customers come from now, what you tried, what works. I did some homework on [Company] and found a couple of things I want to show you, but I do not want to guess what matters, so I would rather hear it from you first.
>
> Then I will tell you honestly if I think we can help. **If I do not think we can, I will say so and point you somewhere better.** I would rather do that than take money for something that will not work.
>
> If I do think we can help, I will show you exactly what I would do and what it costs. Then you tell me yes, no, or 'I need to think about it.' All three are fine.
>
> Sound good?"

**Why every line matters:**
- "I will say so if we cannot help" → instantly you become the only honest person they talked to. Huge trust
- "yes, no, or think about it — all fine" → removes their guard, and quietly gives you permission to ask for a decision at the end
- You set the agenda → you control the call without being pushy

---

### Part 2 — Questions (12 min) — THEY talk

**These are the only questions you need. Ask, then be quiet, then write.**

After every answer say **"Tell me more about that"** or **"Why do you think that is?"** The second answer is always better than the first.

**About the business:**
1. "Walk me through how a new customer finds you today. The most common way."
2. "Roughly how many new [customers/cases/jobs] do you get in a month?"
3. **"What is a new [customer] worth to you on average?"** ← **MOST IMPORTANT QUESTION ON THE CALL.** Everything else is priced against this number
4. "Could you handle 10 more per month if they came tomorrow? Or would something break?"

**About the pain:**
5. "What made you take this call? What is happening right now that made marketing worth thinking about?"
6. "How long has that been a problem?"
7. "What have you already tried?" → then **"What happened?"** ← here you learn who burned them, and what you must not sound like
8. "What did that cost you?"

**About what happens if nothing changes:**
9. "If nothing changes in the next 12 months, what does that look like for the business?"
10. **"Is that acceptable?"** ← then stay silent. Most powerful question in the whole script

**About the decision:**
11. "If we worked together, who else is involved in that decision?"
12. "What would you need to see to feel confident?"
13. "Do you have a budget in mind, or are we working that out together?"

**Take real notes.** Then say back to them: *"So what I am hearing is [X, Y, Z]. Did I get that right?"*

**This one move closes more deals than any pitch.** Because almost nobody has ever correctly repeated their problem back to them.

---

### Part 3 — Show the gap (4 min)

Now show your 3 facts. Do the math **out loud, with them**, using **their** number from question 3.

> "So before the call I looked at a few things. Can I share what I found?
>
> You are position 8 for '[keyword]'. [Competitor] is position 1. That word gets about 480 searches a month in [City].
>
> Position 1 gets about 27% of clicks. Position 8 gets about 1%. So out of 480 people, they get about 130 and you get maybe 5.
>
> You told me a new customer is worth [$800]. Even if only 1 in 10 of those became a job — **that is about $10,000 a month going to them instead of you.**
>
> Second thing: they have 87 Google reviews, you have 11. That is a big part of why the ranking looks like that, and it is also what customers compare.
>
> Does that match what you are seeing?"

**Why this works:** you are not selling SEO. You are showing them a number — **their own number** — leaving their business every month. Now your price is not a cost. It is a small part of a loss they can see.

**Then ask, and stop talking:**

> "So — do you want to fix that, or is now not the right time?"

### Part 4 — What you would do (5 min)

**Do NOT list services.** Prescribe a fix to the problem you just proved.

> "Here is what I would actually do, in order.
>
> **First 30 days** — fix the foundation. Your website is missing [specific thing], and your Google Business Profile is not set up to compete. That is the fastest movement available. I would also set up a review system, because that 11 vs 87 gap is your biggest single lever.

> **Day 90 to 180** — this is when rankings compound and calls start coming steadily. I want to be honest with you: **this is not a 30 day thing.** Anyone who says they will get you page one in 60 days is either lying or about to get your site penalised. Six months is when this looks like a real result.
>

**Notice what the last line does:** it turns your one real strength — personal communication — into a paid feature instead of a free bonus. That is not a trick. Your clients already say it is why they stay. **So charge for it.**

**Then silence.** Count to 10 in your head. It will feel like an hour. Let it.
