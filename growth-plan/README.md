# Mavix Marketing — Growth & Sales Turnaround Plan

**Prepared:** July 2026
**Situation:** 2 years in business. Revenue fell from ~$5,000/mo to ~$1,000/mo. Target: $10,000/mo.
**Constraint:** Minimal budget. Solo operator. Outsourced delivery.

---

## Read this first

You asked how to get from $1,000/mo to $10,000/mo this month.

**Straight answer: $10,000 in *recurring* revenue in 30 days is not happening.** Not because you're bad — because the math doesn't exist. At your current prices ($500 SEO / $300 ads) you'd need 18 new retainer clients in 30 days. Even at a strong close rate that's ~90 sales conversations, which needs ~6,000 well-targeted cold emails landing in inboxes. You've sent 500–600 total. And if you *did* land 18 clients at once, your delivery would collapse and they'd churn inside 90 days — which is exactly what already happened to you once.

**$10,000 in collected cash inside 30–45 days is achievable.** It requires 7 sales, not 20. It's laid out in [`02-money-math.md`](02-money-math.md).

**$10,000/mo recurring is a 90-day plan**, and it's real. It's in [`08-30-60-90.md`](08-30-60-90.md).

---

## The one thing that matters most

You went from $5k to $1k. **That's not an acquisition problem. That's churn.**

You told me the quality is "just fine, not that good" and that clients stay only because you manage them personally with communication. You already know the answer. You're pouring effort into the top of a bucket with a hole in the bottom.

If you add $9k of new clients onto a leaky delivery system, you will be back here in six months, more tired and more broke. **Fix the leak while you fill the bucket, or don't bother.** [`06-onboarding-retention.md`](06-onboarding-retention.md)

## What to do in the next 7 days

Do these in order. Don't skip to the automation.

1. **Day 1 — Free money.** List every past client and every dead lead from the last 24 months. Call/email all of them. Win-back is the highest close rate revenue that exists. You had $5k/mo once; those people already trusted you with money. Script in [`04-channels.md`](04-channels.md#0-reactivation-free-fastest-do-this-today).
2. **Day 1 — Reprice.** New pricing: SEO $1,500, Ads management $1,000, Websites $2,500–4,000. Existing clients get grandfathered for 60 days then a notice. [`02-money-math.md`](02-money-math.md)
3. **Day 2 — Pick one niche.** One industry, US, local-services. Not "everyone." [`01-diagnosis.md`](01-diagnosis.md#problem-2-you-have-no-niche)
4. **Day 3 — Audit your email deliverability before sending one more email.** If your DMARC is `p=none` and you have no one-click unsubscribe, your mail is being *rejected*, not just spam-foldered, under the 2026 Google/Microsoft rules. [`04-channels.md`](04-channels.md#1-cold-email-the-rebuild)
5. **Day 4 — Write and rehearse the sales call script.** Out loud. Ten times. [`05-sales-playbook.md`](05-sales-playbook.md)
6. **Day 5 — Send 20 white-label pitches to other agencies.** One partner = $2,000–5,000/mo from a single sale. You are already structurally a white-label shop — you outsource everything. [`04-channels.md`](04-channels.md#3-white-label--the-highest-leverage-channel-you-are-ignoring)
7. **Day 6–7 — Turn on cold email properly.** Correct infra, tight list, one niche, 3-step sequence.

## On the gray-hat question

You asked about buying an Upwork account and said you'd decide for yourself. Fair. Here's the business answer, not a lecture:

Upwork enforces one-user-one-account and permanently bans bought accounts. The failure mode isn't "you get caught eventually" — it's "you get caught the first time you withdraw money to a name that doesn't match the account, and the funds in escrow go with it." You'd also inherit whatever that seller does with the credentials they kept. And there's a 24-month non-circumvention clause, so a banned account means you can't legally take those clients off-platform either.

The reason not to do it isn't ethics. It's that it's a bad trade: you'd be putting your only reviewed asset in someone else's hands to skip 4–6 weeks of work you can do legitimately. The fast legitimate path to reviews is in [`04-channels.md`](04-channels.md#4-upwork--fix-the-zero-review-problem-in-30-days) and it takes about a month.

I'll tell you where the aggressive-but-legal lines are throughout this doc. There are plenty and they work better.
