## 2. How each part works now

**Reply agent (checks every minute)**
- When someone replies, it reads the whole conversation and writes one short, plain answer.
- It stays quiet on automated mail and on clear "no" replies, and unsubscribes people who ask.
- **"Yes" or any interest:** it answers any question first, then asks 2 simple questions (which services, which area). A few words is enough as an answer.
- **When they answer those:** "I will start preparing your audit", written to fit what they said. The meeting link is offered once, as an option, with "it is not a pitch, just to understand your business". This is the only time the link goes out without them asking.
- **"We already have someone":** one light offer of the free audit, then it leaves it.
- **"Come back in February":** it agrees, and the system writes to them again on that date.
- **Prices:** always US dollars, "starts from $349 a month". "Running since 2019, 500+ clients served."
- **Case studies:** it uses the real ones from your website, picking the closest to their kind of business.

**Follow-ups (when someone goes quiet)**
- They go out on days 1, 2, 4, 8 and 13 after our last email, then a light check-in on day 45 and day 90.
- Each one carries the case study closest to their business and ends with one easy question.
- A few words on calls or bookings appear only where they fit, not every time.
- **No meeting link, ever.**
- **Who gets skipped:**
  - Archived chats (only automated replies and people who said no go there).
  - People who wrote last (the reply agent answers them instead).
  - People who said they already have someone. Only the light day 45 and day 90 check-ins go to them.
  - People waiting on you for their audit.
- **Audits or case studies you send by hand:** the system spots them, and the next follow-up (the next day) asks how they found it.
- **Each person gets theirs at their own time** (see section 3).

**Morning report (Slack, 9am IST)**
- **Numbers:** for yesterday, the last 7 days and the month.
- **Reminders:** yesterday's flags, such as "prepare the audit" or "call this person", shown once.
- **Unanswered emails:** shown once, the morning after.
- **Dates coming up:** this week's "come back on this date" follow-ups.
- **On the 1st of every month:** last month in full, next to normal cold email numbers.

## 3. The timing change, explained simply

**Before:** the follow-ups had one alarm clock, 7:30pm India time, and everyone got theirs at once. 7:30pm India time is:
- 9am in New York
- 2pm in London
- 1am in Sydney

So people in Australia got the email in the middle of the night, and by morning it was buried under other emails.

**Now:**
- The system wakes up every hour and asks: "whose usual time is it right now?" Only those people get their follow-up.
- A person's "usual time" is the time of day they replied to our cold email. SalesBlink records the exact time of every reply, back to 29 July.
- For example, Charese replied in her afternoon in Colorado, so she gets her follow-ups in her afternoon. That is 2:30am India time, but it is the time she is at her inbox.
- If someone has no reply time on record, they get the most common reply time for their list (UK, USA, Canada or Australia).
- Nothing goes out on their Sunday.

**How it helps:** the email arrives when that person is at their inbox, so it sits near the top instead of under the overnight pile. It is a sensible bet, not a proven result. The morning report's reply numbers over the next few weeks will show whether it helps.
