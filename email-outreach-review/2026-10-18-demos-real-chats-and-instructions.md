# Demo chats, real chats and the writer's instructions (18 Oct 2026)

Four parts:
1. 10 demo conversations showing the new rules (made up, clearly marked)
2. Real replies the live system wrote under the new rules, in test mode (nothing sent)
3. Your 10 longest real conversations (written under the **old** rules, before 18 Oct)
4. The exact instructions the email writer and the follow-up writer get now

Email addresses and phone numbers of prospects are removed.

## Part 1: 10 demo conversations under the new rules

These are **made-up examples** that show what the new rules ask for. I wrote them by following the live instructions in Part 4. The businesses and names are invented. Real replies the live system wrote under the new rules (in test mode) are in Part 2.

**Who is who:** THEM is the prospect, US is the automatic reply or follow-up, FARHAN is you writing by hand, and *[Slack]* is what you see in Slack (never sent to the prospect).

---

### Demo 1. Yes, details, audit, then the follow-up after you send it

```text
THEM: yes please, send it over.

US: great, happy to put your free audit together.

so the audit looks at what matters to you, which jobs do you want more of, and which towns do you cover? a few words is enough.

THEM: mostly boiler installs, around leeds and bradford.

US: thanks, i will start preparing your audit for boiler installs in leeds and bradford, and send it over when it is ready.

if you would like to go through it together, a call is optional: https://cal.id/mavixmarketing/strategy-call. it is not a pitch, just a chat to understand your business and where you stand.

[Slack] flag: prepare the audit for leedsheating.co.uk, focus boiler installs in leeds and bradford

(no follow-ups go out now: we owe him the audit. the morning report lists him under "audits you still owe".)

FARHAN (by hand, 2 days later): the audit is attached ...

US (follow-up, the next day): the first thing i would start with is one page for boiler installs in leeds and one for bradford, so people searching there find you before the bigger firms.

if that sounds right, reply yes and i will send the simple next steps.
```
What it shows: two easy questions, the audit email with the booking link offered once, **no follow-ups while the audit is owed**, then one small next step the day after you send it.

### 4c. What the safety check does before anything is sent

**It fixes small slips automatically:**
- capital letters
- a stray "hi" or sign-off
- dashes and exclamation marks
- one long block of text, which it breaks into short paragraphs
- any question asking for their website, which it cuts out

**It holds the email and sends it to you in Slack instead when:**
- there's a link that isn't on our list
- there's a price in dollars before the audit (pay per lead aside)
- it mentions a refund or the "$1,000"
- it suggests a call they didn't ask for
- it uses a currency other than US dollars
- it's too long or too short
- for follow-ups, it gives any price or only chases ("just checking in")
