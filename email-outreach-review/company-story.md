# Mavix Marketing: the company story

Written 23 October 2026. This is the version now used in all three skills: the SEO proposal,
the proposal generator and the client call kit. It is about 130 words; the skills use it shortened to fit the page.

## The story

Mavix Marketing is based in Dubai, with a team of 30+ people across Dubai, Los Angeles and
New Delhi. Everyone who does the work is employed by Mavix, not subcontracted. We do SEO, paid
ads and email, and have done since 2019.

On search we are not a traditional SEO agency. We get businesses found on Google, Google Maps,
and in AI answers such as Google's AI Overviews and ChatGPT.

Every account has one named account manager for the day to day, so you never write to a shared
inbox. Our founder, Mohd Farhan, takes the first call with every new client and joins every
monthly review. Reports lead with enquiries, not traffic, and every number we show comes from
your own accounts, not from a slide we made.
