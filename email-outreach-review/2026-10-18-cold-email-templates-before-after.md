# Cold email templates: before and after (18 Oct 2026)

The 22 templates used by the two running SalesBlink campaigns (Aus/UK/NZ/Sing and USA/Canada). AI Written was not touched. Subject lines are unchanged. Text in `{{ a | b }}` is spintax: SalesBlink picks one option per email.

What changed in every template:
- Spam-trigger words removed: "100%", "full refund", "$1,000" (now "one thousand dollars"), "zero dollars", "no cost", "pay nothing", "no catch", "trick", "secret", "top of your inbox", "just following up / checking in / circling back", and every "!".
- The ask now gets what we need: "Reply yes and tell me the main service you want more customers for". Before, 10 people said yes and never told us what they wanted.
- Simpler words ("show up higher on Google" instead of "improve their Google rankings") and normal contractions (I'll, don't).
- The money back line is the same everywhere: everything they paid back, plus one thousand dollars, if we miss the target we agreed.
- Removed lines that said we might not be able to help them (Farhan's rule: never turn anyone away).

The best performers (1st Follow Up (b) with 35 replies, 2nd Follow Up (b) with 49, Initiail 2, 7, 9 and 10) were changed the least. The ones with 0 to 2 replies were changed more.

The full backup of the old versions is kept, so any template can be put back exactly as it was.
