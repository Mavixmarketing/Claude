# Audit review: why so few prospects reply

Date: 22 October 2026

What I read: the two audits you sent, page by page, as text and as page images.

- **H** = San Antonio Hypnotherapy Center, 22 pages
- **M** = Middleware Associates, 29 pages

Both come from the "mavix-seo-proposal" skill. Across about 17 audits sent, only about 3 prospects wrote back.

I read them the way a busy owner reads them: on a phone, between jobs, looking for three things. Did they look at **my** business? What will it cost? What do I do next?

The research is good. The H search table (p5) and the M women-owned point (p3, p11) are sharp and specific. The problems are length, trust slips and a weak ending.

Points are ranked by how much they hurt the deal. The biggest problem is first.

---

## 1. Fake-looking testimonials make the whole document feel like a template

**Where:** H p13, M p20.

**What is wrong:** two of the six cards say "Director, client name to confirm" and "Marketing Manager, client name to confirm". The page above them says "Real clients, real names". A careful reader stops trusting everything after this point. On M, "Mohammed Ahmed, Managing Director" has no company name.

**Fix:** only print testimonials whose name and company you have confirmed. Four confirmed cards in a 2 by 2 grid beat six cards where two are placeholders. Never ship a page that says "to confirm". *(Applied to the skill.)*

## 2. Too long, and the value is buried

**Where:** H is 22 pages and M is 29. The price is on H p17 and M p24. The ask is on the last page.

**What is wrong:** on a phone, nobody gets to page 17. Before reaching any value, the reader has to get past a cover, a contents page and an "about your business" table. M then adds eight pages of technical audit (p5 to p12). After the price come four pages that repeat each other (see point 8).

**Fix:**
- Make page 2 "The short version": three findings, the plan in one line, the 90-day target and the guarantee.
- Cut the contents page.
- Aim for under 20 pages. *(Applied: the new skill makes 19 pages instead of 21.)*

## 3. The ending asks them to do work, and has no easy yes

**Where:** H p22, M p29.

**What is wrong:** the ending says "Reply with a plan and we send the one page agreement today". The prospect has to go back to the pricing page and choose between three plans, and there is no "just talk to me" option. The "Reply and we begin" button does nothing when tapped. H had already replied with small questions, so a 15-minute call is exactly what that kind of prospect needs.

**Fix:** end on one page called "Your plan, your price, reply yes to start". It shows:
- the one recommended plan and its monthly price
- the 90-day target
- the first three things we do
- two ways to say yes: reply "yes" to the email, or book a 15-minute walkthrough at https://cal.id/mavixmarketing/strategy-call

Make both buttons tappable links. *(Applied.)*

## 4. Case studies do not match and their numbers contradict themselves

**Where:** H p14 to p16, M p21 to p23.

**What is wrong:**
- **Headlines say "monthly" for totals.** The headline says "326,000 monthly clicks", but the Search Console panel underneath shows 326K **total** over 18 months. The red tile says "31K monthly clicks". "From 640 to 9,940 monthly clicks" also sits above 9.94K total clicks.
- **The names are invented.** "Halcyon Family Law, Manchester, 11 solicitors" and the others do not match the case studies on the website. A prospect who checks the website will not find these firms.
- **The examples do not fit.** A hypnotherapist gets a UK law firm, a supplements store and a trade distributor.

**Fix:** use only the real website case studies, with the website's names, numbers and time periods, and the URL on the page. Say "total clicks over 7 months", never "monthly". *(The updated skill already does this. Your earlier update made it, and I kept it.)*

## 5. The "Quick wins" page teaches them to do it without you

**Where:**
- H p11: the exact Google description text to paste in, the address fix and the review ask.
- M p18: the exact DMARC record and title to paste in.
- Also M p11 ("Every fix on it is free and inside the first month") and M p5 ("the two fastest to fix").

**What is wrong:** you show the problem, then hand over the fix and tell them it takes 5 to 20 minutes. The owner fixes three things and feels done. Also, your first three weeks of paid work are now "free".

**Fix:**
- Show what is wrong and what it costs them. Keep the "how" for paid work.
- If you want to give something away, give **one** small tip in the email or at the end of a call, not a whole page.
- Cut the quick wins page. *(Applied: it is not in the skill's section list, and the skill now bans DIY fix instructions.)*

## 6. The pricing page looks cheap and confusing

**Where:** H p17, M p24, plus the targets page (H p10, M p17).

**What is wrong:**
- **"Was 799 USD" looks like a sales gimmick.** It is crossed out under 549, and a prospect cannot check it.
- **The jump makes no sense.** Prices go from 549 to 1,699 and nothing on the page explains the 3x gap.
- **The Starter card leads with what it lacks.** Its biggest box says "No guarantee on this plan".
- **The guarantee is worded three different ways** ("2 of 10 agreed searches", "the 90-day target", "every fee back").
- **The targets look unfinished.** They show "TBC → 2 of 10".

**Fix:**
- Use the new prices: $999, then $1,349 (recommended), then $1,999. Remove the "was" line.
- Show the guarantee as a small tag on Growth and Premium only, and describe it the same way everywhere.
- Fill in the real 90-day target before sending. Never print "TBC".
- Use a cleaner, more confident layout. *(Applied.)*

## 7. Lines that should not be in a cold document

- **H p4:** "Dr. M.D. Witt reads like a medical degree … A careful reader may feel misled." In a first contact, this tells the owner they are misleading people. Raise it gently on a call, or not at all.
- **H p6 and p22:** "Start this week, rank by New Year." **M p29:** "be found before budgets are set." These are ranking promises you cannot keep, and they contradict your own guarantee page. Say "start this week and the new pages are live before the January rush".
- **H p12 and M p19:** "Google Ads" chips on the cover and the "1,000 USD Google ad credit" are not part of an SEO deal. They distract and blur the offer.
- **M p19:** "Google Partner status means Google audits the accounts we run" overstates what Partner status is. Keep it to "Google Partner".
- **H p19:** "You asked us to tell you if and what." This is a broken sentence and reads like a template slipped. Proofread the lead line of every page.
- **Your skills disagree with each other.** The call kit says "headquartered in Dubai, team of just over thirty", while the proposal says "a small team run by Mohd Farhan… nobody behind him". A prospect who gets both will notice. You need to pick one story. *(Not changed. This is your call, see "Needs your decision" below.)*

## 8. Four pages at the back say the same thing

**Where:** H p19 to p22 and M p26 to p29 (Others vs us, How we work together, Reporting, After you say yes).

**What is wrong:** "Same working day", "month to month, 30 days notice" and "your accounts stay yours" each appear four or five times. The "Others vs us" page ends with "None of this makes us special." That is filler.

**Fix:**
- Make one "How we work together" page: channels, reporting, ownership and leaving.
- Cut "Others vs us".
- Put the first three steps on the final close page.

*(Applied: three pages cut.)*

## 9. M is written for an SEO person, not a business owner

**Where:** M p5 to p12.

**What is wrong:** these pages are full of terms like letter grades, canonical tags, H1s, llms.txt, Moz PA, SPF/DMARC and WebP. The owner cannot judge any of it, and much of it is fine ("GOOD, keep as is"). It also makes the document 8 pages longer.

**Fix:** use one scorecard page in plain words, then the top five problems, each with what it costs them. Offer the full technical list on the call or as a separate file.

## 10. The AI search angle is missing or buried

**Where:** H only says "Written to be quoted by ChatGPT too" (p9). M p12 has an AI page, but it is a checklist of technical items.

**Fix:** add one page, "Found on Google, Maps and AI answers". It says:
- Mavix is not a traditional SEO agency.
- It gets businesses found on Google, Google Maps, AI Overviews and AI Mode, and assistants like ChatGPT.

Use only two facts:
- Google says AI Overviews reach over 2 billion people a month (Alphabet, July 2025).
- Pew Research (July 2025) found people clicked a normal result on 8% of searches with an AI summary, against 15% without.

Never promise a spot in an AI answer. *(Applied to the proposal and the call kit.)*

## 11. Smaller things

- **Half-empty pages:** H p12, p13 and p17 to p22, and M p25 to p29. That is a lot of swiping on a phone. Merge thin pages.
- **The H p5 chart is unreadable at phone size**, because its labels are tiny. Use a table, or fewer bars with bigger labels.
- **Section numbers jump** ("Case studies, 12 to 14", "The SEO audit, 03 to 10"). This only matters if you keep a contents page.
- **Old terms are still printed.** "Month to month, 30 days notice" (H p17 and p20, M p24 and p27) needs to change to the new terms: no long contract, no time limit, and the agreement only ties Mavix, to deliver.
