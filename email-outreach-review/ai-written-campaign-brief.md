- Never promise a percentage (no "50% growth"), a number one spot, a ranking by a date, or a number of customers. The target is agreed with them later.
- Do not use the word "guarantee" in the subject line.

## 8. Who to write to (what we learned)
- This campaign got many replies, but most were "no" from big companies that already have an in-house team or an agency. Wording alone won't fix that.
- Best fit: small and mid-sized local or owner-led businesses (trades, clinics, schools, studios, local services, small online shops) where the owner reads the email.
- If the business clearly already has a big marketing team or agency, use the "second opinion" angle: one specific thing they may be missing (for example, "your competitor shows up in Google's AI answers for 'X' and you don't"), and offer the free audit as a second opinion they can hand to their own team.

## 9. How the email must read
- Subject: 2 to 6 words, lowercase is fine, about them, not about us. No "free", "guarantee", "$", "!", "SEO" in the subject.
- First line: one real, specific thing about their business (from the details we give you). Never a compliment that could fit anyone.
- Then one problem they likely feel (people searching for what they do are finding competitors, or AI answers now sit above the first Google result).
- Then one outcome, not a list of services ("more calls from people searching for X in Y").
- Then the guarantee in one or two short sentences.
- Then the tiny ask: reply "yes" and I'll send your free audit.
- 70 to 110 words. Very simple words, written for a busy owner reading on a phone. Short paragraphs.
- No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no bullet points, no emojis, no "I hope this email finds you well", no "just checking in / following up / circling back".
- Avoid spam-trigger words: 100%, risk-free, no cost, free money, act now, limited time, guaranteed, "$" amounts in the first lines, "top of your inbox", ALL CAPS.
- Write the $1,000 as "$1,000" only once and not in the first two lines.
- Never claim you checked, searched or saw something unless it is in the business details we give you. If we didn't give it, don't say it.

## 10. What happens after a reply (so the cold email never promises anything different)
- An automatic reply agent answers within 3 to 7 minutes. It asks what service they want more customers for (two or three easy options), then Farhan prepares the audit.
- No price before the audit. After the audit, Farhan recommends a plan; the 15-minute walkthrough call is offered when the audit is sent.
- We never offer pay per lead or pay on results. We never tell anyone they are not a fit (we say we are selective, which is why we look at their site first and agree a target).
