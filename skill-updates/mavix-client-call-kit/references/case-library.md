## How to choose three, in this order

**(a) Same service as the kit. Absolute.**
An SEO prospect sees SEO case studies, an ads prospect sees ads case studies, and neither one ever sees the other kind. It does not bend for anything.

**(b) Never a direct rival.**
Not the prospect's own trade in the prospect's own town. The location of a case study is whatever the website says it is. Never move a case study to another city or country to make this work; pick a different one instead.

**(c) Closest industry match.**
Work down: same trade, then same shape of business, then same kind of buying decision. The website's own guide:

| Prospect | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Accountants, bookkeepers, tax, lawyers, insurance, consultants | Financial advisory firm |
| Clinics, therapists, chiropractors, med spas, health | My Favorite Therapists, Dental clinic or Orthopaedic clinic group |
| Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, roofers, cleaners, repair, septic, builders and other trades | Plumbing company or Construction management firm |
| Schools, courses, training, driving schools, gyms, studios | Martial arts academy or Exam preparation platform |
| Pets, tours, travel, local services | All Dogs Rock |
| Online stores and products | Online home goods store, Skincare brand or La Vela Coffee |
| Property and real estate | Real estate brokerage |
| Software | HR software company or Education software company |

**(d) Three different points, not the same point three times.**
A set that says "more clicks", "more clicks" and "more clicks" is weaker than one that says "a local business can win its own town", "this works at scale" and "search started bringing sales".

**Prefer a case study with a dashboard** when the fit is otherwise equal. A real screenshot on page 9 is stronger than words.

## The rule on numbers

**Where a dashboard exists.** Use its figures and embed the screenshot as it is. Never recreate it, never crop it, never recolour it, never redraw it. Never state a number the screenshot does not show or the website does not state.

**Where no dashboard exists.** Quote the website's result, exactly as written, and link the page. Do not draw a chart that would need made-up data.

**Invent nothing.** Not a figure, not a percentage, not a month, not a quote, not a city. If a gap needs filling and the live page does not fill it, write it in general terms that are true of how Mavix works, or cut it, and tell Farhan in the chat summary.

## How to build one, once the three are chosen

A4 portrait, same house style as the review PDF. See `references/build.md` for the toolkit.

1. **Fetch the live case study page** at its URL. Everything on pages 2 to 9 comes from that page, from the dashboard, or from general statements that are true of how Mavix works.
2. **Cut any page the live page cannot support.** The nine-page structure is the maximum, not a quota. A competitor table, a keyword map, area pages or a before-and-after listing only go in when the live case study page shows them. A shorter true document beats a full invented one.
3. **The cover** carries the website's name for the client, the industry as the website states it, the location only if the website states it, and the case study URL in the footer.
4. **If the live page cannot be fetched**, do not build the document from the one-line summary. Send the case study link instead, and say so in the chat summary.

**There are two page structures. Use the one that matches the service.**

| # | SEO case study | Ads case study |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Cover |
| 2 | The client, as the website describes it | The client, as the website describes it |
| 3 | Goals | Goals |
| 4 | Competitors, compared (only if the live page shows them) | What the money was doing (only if the live page shows it) |
| 5 | The work, numbered stages | What we changed, numbered stages |
| 6 | What people type, mapped to pages (only if the live page shows it) | The audiences and the offer |
| 7 | The pages themselves (only if the live page shows them) | Where the click landed (only if the live page shows it) |
| 8 | The listing, before and after (only if the live page shows it) | The account, before and after (only if the live page shows it) |
| 9 | Results: the real dashboard or the website's result, plus the URL | Results: the real dashboard or the website's result, plus the URL |

Build scripts: `assets/build_case_study_seo.py` with `assets/case_content_seo.py`, or `assets/build_case_study_ads.py` with `assets/case_content_ads.py`. **The content files are layout examples only.** Their client names, places and stories are retired inventions. Copy the structure, never a sentence of the copy.

### Putting the real dashboard on page 9

```python
from dashboards import results_panel
inner = results_panel(
    "g2",                                  # only a key matched to a website case study
    title="Google Ads account, one month", # the website's timeframe
    note="Taken straight from the client's account. Full case study: mavixmarketing.com/case-studies/lead-gen-volume",
    highlight=2,                           # which tile gets the lime bar
    service="ads")                         # refuses if the key is the wrong service
```

`service=` is a guard, not decoration. Pass it every time. It throws rather than let an SEO dashboard reach an ads kit.
