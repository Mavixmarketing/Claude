# The SEO review PDF

A4 portrait, 12-14 pages, rendered at 2480x3508 (300dpi), assembled with img2pdf. This is screen-shared to the prospect and emailed after.

Do not build the layout from scratch. `assets/` holds the working toolkit and a complete worked example — see `references/build.md` before writing a line of code.

## Tone

The prospect does good work. Every finding is **money currently on the table**, never incompetence. They should finish it feeling there is an obvious opportunity, not that they have been told off.

## Pages

1. **Cover** — their name, city, service, date. A fact table and a contents list. The headline in one line: same rating, fewer people see you.
2. **What we looked at** — the method, plainly. Builds credibility that this is real work and not a template.
3. **The headline finding** — the comparison that carries the whole call, as the hero chart. For SEO that is usually the review-count bar chart with ratings alongside and a yes/no column for the box at the top of Google. Add a callout that separates quality from volume, so it lands as *"you are as good, you are just less visible."*
4. **Their listing** — what is filled in and what is not, as two columns. Any address mismatch.
5. **Where they rank** — the keyword table, page-one rankings visually separated from the misses. Every column explained in words.
6. **The traffic they get is not the traffic they want** — the click estimate as a hero stat, with a position band chart showing where those rankings actually sit.
7. **The open ground** — the easy wins. Difficulty meters with volume and cost per click beside them. Framed as opportunity.
8. **The towns they are missing** — hub and spoke, covered against uncovered.
9. **Their website** — the "how much does it cost" problem, side by side against a competitor who answers it.
10. **What we would do** — numbered stages, then a callout that says Mavix is not a traditional SEO agency: the same work gets them found on Google, Google Maps, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, and assistants like ChatGPT. Only the two allowed AI facts from `facts.md`, with sources. Never promise a spot in an AI answer.
11. **The first six months** — a vertical timeline with date chips. Honest about how long rankings take.
12. **How we work** — the weekly, monthly and always cards, then the agency-problems table.
13. **Pricing** — the three tiers from `facts.md` ($749 / $1,800 / $2,500, or the price already quoted to them), Growth recommended, the only "was" price is Starter's (normally $1,049), the guarantee callout (our money back guarantee, Growth and Premium: if the target agreed in writing is not hit within 180 days, the client chooses everything back or free work until it is hit; the old terms only for someone already given them), the terms strip (no setup fee, no long contract, no time limit, every account in your name). End the page with the 15-minute walkthrough link for anyone reading it after the call.
14. **Hiring someone, or using us** — two panels. Qualitative, no invented salary figures.

**This page list is for an SEO kit only.** An ads kit uses the fourteen pages in `ads-track.md` section 5 instead — that list overrides this one completely, including the pricing and guarantee page. Do not merge the two.

## Rules

- **Every page carries a chart, table, diagram or callout** that tells the story on its own. A reader skimming for ten seconds should get it.
- **Every figure traces to the research.** Any derived number is labelled as derived.
- Never promise a ranking or a lead count.
- Never name another Mavix client.
- No placeholders ("TBC", "to confirm"), no paste-ready fixes they can do alone, no ranking promise with a date.
- Second person throughout. "You have 42 reviews."
- Explain every number the moment it appears.

## Before shipping

Look at all 14 rendered pages. Nothing clipped, nothing overflowing, no box left lopsided by short copy, every chart aligned with its caption. Fix and re-render until clean.
