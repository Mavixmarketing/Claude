# Voice

Everything is written the way Farhan would say it to someone across a table.

## The test

Read it out loud. If it snags, rewrite it. If a business owner would not use the word, cut it.

## Rules

- **Short sentences.** One idea each.
- **Everyday words.** "shows up" not "appears in results". "the box at the top of Google" not "the map pack".
- **Second person in client documents.** "You have 42 reviews", not "The company has 42 reviews".
- **Explain every number as it appears.** Never let a figure sit alone. "480 people a month type that into Google. You are not on the first page for it."
- **Kind, not blunt.** Findings are money on the table, never failure. The reader should finish feeling there is an obvious opportunity, not that they have been told off.
- **Headings say the thing.** "You have fewer reviews than the three above you" beats "The headline finding".

## Banned outright

**Jargon:** optimise · visibility · presence · authority · footprint · ecosystem · engagement · conversion · funnel · indexed · crawl · on-page · off-page · technical SEO · schema · canonical · anchor text · domain authority · CTR · ROAS · CPA · SERP · CRO · KPI · ROI · attribution · map pack (say "the box at the top of Google")

**Words:** leverage · utilise · delve · robust · seamless · streamline · elevate · unlock · supercharge · empower · foster · cultivate · myriad · plethora · paradigm · holistic · synergy · bespoke · meticulous · intricate · nuanced · multifaceted · invaluable · transformative · embark · relentless · groundbreaking · revolutionary · unparalleled · world-class · best-in-class · top-notch · cutting-edge · state-of-the-art · game-changer · renowned · harness · illuminate · bolster · commence · furthermore · moreover · consequently · subsequently · thereby · unwavering · turnkey · value-add · pivotal · beacon · cornerstone · gateway · testament

**Phrases:** "at the end of the day" · "circle back" · "touch base" · "moving forward" · "in today's landscape" · "drive results" · "solutions provider" · "low-hanging fruit" · "digital transformation" · "growth hacking" · "10x" · "no-brainer" · "crushing it" · "here's the thing" · "the truth is" · "that being said" · "in order to" · "deep dive" · "great question" · "rest assured" · "data-driven" · "results-driven" · "we help businesses grow" · "take your business to the next level" · "do you have any questions?"

**Instead of "great question":** "Fair question."
**Instead of "do you have any questions?":** "What else do you want me to show you?"
